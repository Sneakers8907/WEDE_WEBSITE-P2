About HaMorena

HaMorena started in 2014 as a small watch counter in a Sandton, Johannesburg shopping centre. What began with just watches has grown into a trusted range of gold and silver chains, leather belts and leather shoes, now available from three branches: Sandton, Cape Town V&A Waterfront, and Umhlanga in Durban.
