Our History

HaMorena started in 2014 as a small watch counter in a Sandton, Johannesburg shopping centre. The two owners wanted to sell good quality men's accessories instead of the usual mall chain stock. It grew from just watches into gold and silver chains, leather belts and leather shoes, and now has three branches: Sandton, Cape Town V&A Waterfront, and Umhlanga in Durban.

Our Mission

To give South African men access to good quality, long lasting accessories without having to import everything or pay overseas prices.

Our Vision

To become a trusted online store for men's accessories in South Africa, known for good service and products that last.

Our Stores

  • Sandton, Johannesburg
  • V&A Waterfront, Cape Town
  • Umhlanga, Durban

Who We Serve

Our customers are men aged 25-50 with a steady income, gift shoppers looking for birthdays, anniversaries and Father's Day, watch collectors who like to browse online before visiting a store, and young professionals who prefer a few good quality items over lots of cheap ones.
